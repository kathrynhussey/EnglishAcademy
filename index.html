FAQ

Common questions

A few practical details before we begin.

Are the lessons suitable for beginners?

Yes. Lessons are adapted to your current level, from beginner to advanced.

How do online lessons work?

Lessons take place by video call. You receive the learning materials and clear guidance before or during each session.

Can lessons focus on my profession?

Yes. Vocabulary, scenarios and documents can be adapted to your role and sector.

What happens if I need to reschedule?

Rescheduling terms will be explained clearly in your contract before lessons begin.

Can my company pay for the course?

Invoices can be issued. Whether your employer can fund the course depends on its own training policy and available budget.

Are courses eligible for CPF funding?

No. Courses are not offered directly through CPF because the activity does not currently hold Qualiopi certification.
